1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached to this submission: recorded on an iPhone with the latest iOS, starting with the app launch. The app has no accounts, no login, no user-generated content shared with others and no paid content.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Wattspur is a free logbook for e-bike, bicycle and motorcycle rides, made by an individual developer. It is for riders who want to see and keep their own rides without a cloud service account: the GPS track on a map, elevation profile, speed, power, cadence, heart rate, lean angle, and totals per week, month and bike. It records rides with the iPhone's GPS and imports FIT and GPX files from e-bike systems, bike computers and other apps. All data stays on the device and, if the user wants, in their own iCloud. No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No sign-in, no setup, no credentials needed.
- Launch: the Overview tab shows an anonymized sample ride until there is a real ride, so every screen has content right away.
- Record: tap "Start Ride" on the Overview. Allow location "While Using the App" and Motion & Fitness. Walk or ride a few hundred metres, then tap "Stop". The ride appears in "Rides". While recording, location is used in the background (screen locked) and a Live Activity shows the ride; nothing runs in the background when not recording.
- Import: in "Rides" tap "+" and pick a FIT or GPX file, or share a GPX file to Wattspur from Files or another app. Any GPX file with a track works. A sample FIT file (anonymized e-bike ride) to import: https://github.com/hops-1/wattspur/raw/main/App/SampleRide.fit
- Ride details: tap a ride for map, charts, lean angle and the ride's figures. "Share" exports a GPX file.
- Map: the "Map" tab shows all rides on one map. Map styles are under "More > Maps"; Apple Maps is the default.
- Widgets: home and lock screen widgets show distance and the last ride.
- iCloud: "More > iCloud" syncs the rides with the user's Mac (Wattspur for macOS) via the user's private CloudKit database.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple: MapKit (default map), Core Location, Core Motion (barometer altitude, lean angle), CloudKit private database (optional sync), ActivityKit, WidgetKit.
- Optional map styles, chosen by the user: OpenStreetMap tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org, OpenStreetMap Foundation), CyclOSM (tiles by OpenStreetMap France), OpenTopoMap. Only tiles on screen are loaded, with attribution on every map, following each operator's tile usage policy.
- No own server, no analytics, no login or payment service, no AI service. No data is collected.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. It is localized in English and German and uses the device's units (km or miles).
