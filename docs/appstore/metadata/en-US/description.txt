Wattspur shows your rides by e-bike, bike, motorcycle and car: distance, time, climb, speed and, if recorded, power, heart rate and cadence. No account, no ads, no tracking.

RECORD ON IPHONE
• GPS recording with one tap, also with the screen locked.
• Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with pause.
• Altitude from the barometer, much steadier than GPS altitude alone.
• Lean angle, lateral acceleration and road surface from the motion sensors.
• Map in the direction of travel, track colored by altitude, profile grows as you ride.
• Portrait and landscape for your mount.
• A suggested name from start, destination and time.

IN THE CAR
• CarPlay: start, pause and end a recording, choose the vehicle, a few values on one panel.
• Auto-Start through an automation in the Shortcuts app: Wattspur waits as "Ready" and begins when you drive off.
• Automatic end when disconnected or after standing still.

IMPORT
• FIT and GPX files from the Files app, several at once.
• "Share to Wattspur" from other apps and trackers.
• Heart rate, cadence and power from the file, if it has them.
• Wattspur spots duplicate rides and asks what to do.

VIEW
• Every ride with map, elevation profile and one page per metric.
• Touch the chart: value and place at any point.
• Map of all rides as lines or a heat map.
• Highlights with your year, records and goals.
• "This Week" widget for the Home Screen.
• Several bikes and vehicles, each with its own color.

ON IPAD
• Sidebar, with the list and the ride side by side.
• Analysis by period, compare and merge rides.
• The iPad does not record rides. Recording, Auto-Start and CarPlay are on iPhone.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
There is no Wattspur server and no account. Your rides stay on your devices and in your own iCloud while "Sync with iCloud" is on. That is the default on iPhone and iPad, and you can turn it off. Wattspur collects no data.

Back up and restore the library to a folder you choose, also automatically once a week.

GOOD TO KNOW
Wattspur shows distances and speeds in km and km/h. GPS running in the background can drain the battery considerably faster. Use iPhone and CarPlay only when it is safe.
